Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection
Mission 3 Reflection

Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, the cloud platform that impressed me the most is Amazon Web Services (AWS). I was impressed by the wide variety of services that AWS offers for different types of users and organizations. It provides services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other cloud requirements. By studying AWS, I gained a better understanding of how cloud platforms provide flexible resources without requiring organizations to maintain all of their physical infrastructure.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three are major public cloud platforms that provide computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and other services. They also allow organizations to scale their resources according to their needs and provide tools for managing cloud infrastructure. Although their services have different names and features, they can solve many of the same business and technical problems.

Business requirements have a significant impact on the selection of a cloud platform. A company should consider its budget, existing technology, performance requirements, security needs, scalability, and the type of applications it will use. For example, a business that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning may consider GCP. Therefore, the best cloud provider is not always the most popular one, but the provider that best matches the organization's requirements.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, I learned that choosing a cloud provider requires careful analysis. I learned that technical decisions should be aligned with the actual needs and goals of the client. Comparing different providers and analyzing various business scenarios helped me improve my decision-making and problem-solving skills.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, comparison tables, client recommendations, and Linux investigation results. My portfolio is now more organized and demonstrates my knowledge of cloud computing concepts and my ability to document technical work using Markdown.
